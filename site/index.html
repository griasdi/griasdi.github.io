A voice engine built for long sessions: echo cancellation, automatic gain, a noise model that eats your mechanical keyboard, and a jitter buffer for every speaker. Push-to-talk, speech detection or voice activation, your call.

Everything your crew does on a Saturday night.

Text channels, voice channels, calls and direct messages in one app. All of it encrypted, all of it fast.

  • Text channels and DMs

    Markdown, replies, reactions, mentions and @here. Edit your last message with the up arrow.

  • Voice channels and calls

    Hop in and out of voice rooms, or call someone directly. Moderators can mute for everyone.

  • Screen sharing

    A screen, a window, a capture card or a camera, with its sound. Into the call or into a channel.

  • Files of any size

    Too big for the server? It goes from member to member in 1 MiB pieces, each one checked, from everyone who has it.

  • Watch together

    Videos play right in the chat, even while they are still arriving. YouTube included.

  • Roles that hold

    Ranked roles, private channels, owners. Every change is signed and checked by every member.

  • Updates itself

    New builds install on their own. A restart keeps your place, your call and your screen share.

  • Keyboard first

    Ctrl+K opens the command palette. Everything else has a key too.

No accounts. Just keys.

You are a key pair on your device, not a username with a password reset. Text is encrypted with MLS (RFC 9420), voice and screens with SFrame (RFC 9605), all over QUIC. Servers order, store and forward ciphertext. That is all they can do.

  • Join with a link. No sign-up, no e-mail, no phone number.
  • Each device holds its own key, certified by yours.
  • New members and devices get the keys to read history from the others, not from the server.

Smart where you sit. Not on a server.

griasdi runs its models on your own computer, on its own inference engine. What you say is never sent anywhere to be analysed.

“Felix joined.” Said by my own computer.

  • Noise suppression

    Three strengths, from “quiet room” to “someone is drilling next door”. On the processor, in real time.

  • Speech detection

    Your microphone opens when you speak, not when you type, chew or breathe heavily into a boss fight.

  • Live transcripts

    Opt in, and your graphics card writes along. Captions are posted encrypted to the channel, and call transcripts vanish when the call ends.

  • Spoken cues

    A voice that says who joined, by name, in ten voices. Opt in, runs locally.

  • Numbers, not promises

    Every model has a Details view. The two on your microphone show their median and slowest 1 % for every second, and noise suppression falls back to the standard one if it gets too slow.

MCP server for your agents

Let an AI agent list your conversations, read and send messages as you, and read the live transcript of your call. Listens on 127.0.0.1 only, refuses browsers, every request needs your token. The plug in the title bar hands you the configuration:

{
  "mcpServers": {
    "griasdi": {
      "type": "http",
      "url": "http://127.0.0.1:13337/mcp",
      "headers": { "Authorization": "Bearer …" }
    }
  }
}
